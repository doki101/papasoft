MockNavi – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 October 2026

MockNavi (“the App”) is an Android application that provides GPS mock location simulation for developers and testers working on navigation and map-based applications. This policy explains what data the App handles, which third-party services it uses, and what leaves your device.

1. Who is responsible

The App is published by papaSoft / Gyorgy Keszte, Hungary.

Contact: contact@papasoft.hu

2. Data stored only on your device

MockNavi has no user account or login system, and the developer operates no server that receives your data. Settings, saved routes, favorites and simulated positions are stored locally on your device. Uninstalling the App removes them.

3. Data sent to third-party services

Some features require the App to contact external services. This happens only when you use the feature:

  • Route planning (OpenRouteService, operated by HeiGIT): when you calculate a route, the coordinates of the start point, end point and any intermediate points, together with the selected travel profile, are sent to the OpenRouteService API. Your IP address and the time of the request are technically transmitted as well. Privacy information: openrouteservice.org/privacy-policy
  • Place search (OpenStreetMap Nominatim, operated by the OpenStreetMap Foundation): the text you type into the search field is sent to nominatim.openstreetmap.org to find matching locations. Your IP address, the time of the request and the App’s identifier (User-Agent) are transmitted as well. Privacy information: osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  • Map display (Google Maps SDK): the App loads map tiles from Google. Google may process technical data such as your IP address and device information, independently of the developer, under Google’s privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Map data is provided by OpenStreetMap contributors.

The developer does not receive, see or store the data sent to these services, and does not use it for advertising or analytics.

4. Permissions

  • Mock location: the App can act as the mock location provider that you select in Android’s Developer options. The simulated position is delivered to the Android system and to other apps on your device.
  • Location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION): used to show your device position and to center the map.
  • Bluetooth (BLUETOOTH_CONNECT, nearby devices): optional feature to send the simulated position as NMEA data to a Bluetooth device you choose. The connection is direct between your phone and that device.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) and foreground service: to show and control the running simulation.
  • Internet and network state: to load maps, calculate routes and search.

5. Data sharing

MockNavi does not sell your data and does not share it with advertisers. Data is transmitted only to the service providers listed in section 3, solely to provide the requested feature (route calculation, search, map display).

6. In-app purchases

The Pro upgrade is processed by Google Play Billing. The developer does not see or store your payment information.

7. Your rights

Since the developer does not store personal data about you, there is normally nothing to access or delete on the developer’s side. You can remove all locally stored data by clearing the App’s data or uninstalling it. For questions about your data, contact us at the address above. You may also lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority; in Hungary this is the NAIH (naih.hu).

8. Children

The App is not directed at children under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
