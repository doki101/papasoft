Elderly SOS

User information

Elderly SOS is a simple safety app for older people and for the family members who worry about them.

Using a mobile phone can be hard for older adults, especially in an emergency: unlocking the screen, opening the phone app, picking the right contact and starting the call. We created this app to make asking for help easier in exactly those moments.

Pressing the power button three times in quick succession calls a preset phone number. The call starts automatically, and the recipient also receives an SMS with a Google Maps link showing the current location. This works even when the phone is locked, the screen is dark, or another app is open.

How it works

  1. Enter the number to call and the phone’s own number (it appears in the SMS text).
  2. Tap the Start button and allow the permissions the app asks for.
  3. That’s it. From now on, three quick presses of the power button start the alert.

How to use it

Once the app is installed and set up, it needs no further attention on the phone. It runs in the background and watches for power button presses.

When the power button is pressed three times in quick succession, the app:

  • calls the preset number (on speakerphone),
  • sends an SMS to the same number with the date, the time and the phone’s exact position as a map link.

The call and the SMS work with the screen off, with the phone locked, and while another app is in use. The user only has to press the power button three times, quickly, one after another.

After the phone restarts (for example after a flat battery has been recharged), the app starts automatically and is ready right away.

Installation and first launch

The settings only need to be completed once, the first time you open the app after installing it.

  1. The app opens with a logo animation, then explains that a few system settings are needed to use it.
  2. After you tap OK, the app guides you through the required settings.
  3. Next, the phone number screen appears. Enter the number to call: in an emergency the app calls this number and also sends it an SMS with a Google Maps link to your location. Tapping the link shows the exact position of the person asking for help.
  4. Then enter the phone’s own number as well.
  5. Finally, an information message explains that after you tap OK the window closes and disappears from the screen, but the app keeps running in the background, watching for three quick presses of the power button. When that happens, the app automatically calls the number you entered.

  • When the trial ends, the emergency call function switches off until you buy the Pro version.
  • If you want to keep using the app, you can buy Pro inside the app with a single tap (Upgrade to Pro).

Who it is for

  • Older people who live alone
  • Anyone who finds a touchscreen hard to use in an emergency
  • Family members and caregivers who set up the phone

Good to know

  • The SMS contains the date, the time, the phone’s own number and a map link. If the position is not available, the SMS is still sent, with a note saying so.
  • The app asks for these permissions: Phone (to place the call), SMS (to send the message), Location, including in the background (so a position is available even when the screen is off), and an exemption from battery optimization (so the system does not shut the app down).
  • The app and its interface are available in Hungarian and English, depending on the phone’s language.
  • No ads, no account, no registration.

Important

Elderly SOS does not replace the official emergency services, and it does not call 112 on its own: it calls the number you preset.

The call and the SMS are charged by your mobile carrier. How well the alert works also depends on signal strength, the battery, the phone’s settings and the manufacturer’s battery management.

Always test the app after setting it up, and check it again after phone updates.
